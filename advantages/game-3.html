3-Permanent Advantage - Passed Pawn in the Middlegame

Steinitz's Advantages: 3-Permanent Advantage - Passed Pawn in the Middlegame · 22 January 2019

It is obvious that White's passed d-pawn is a strong asset in this position. The plan is to try to promote the pawn or threaten to promote. White can also use the advanced passed pawn to bring his pieces in the enemy territory as he has a space advantage due to the pawn as well.
