6-Permanent Advantage - Pawn islands

Steinitz's Advantages: 6-Permanent Advantage - Pawn islands · 23 January 2019

As can be seen, Black has 4 pawn islands. These are weak because they lack the protection from pawns. White also has two pawn islands, but these are stronger than Black's since they include more pawns that can protect each other. These pawn islands represent targets for White, and Black, in turn, has to protect them with pieces. This means that Black's pieces will be tied down to defensive roles.
