6. Questions for Each Phase

Use phase-specific questions to keep your thinking focused. They are prompts, not substitutes for checking tactics.

Opening

  1. Am I controlling the center?
  2. Are my pieces developed?
  3. Can I castle?

Middlegame

  1. What weaknesses can I attack?
  2. Which of my pieces can I improve?
  3. What does my opponent want, and can I restrict it?

Endgame

  1. Which pawns are weak and attackable?
  2. Can I create or advance a passed pawn?
  3. Can I activate my king?

Always add the safety check

In every phase, ask what the opponent threatens and whether your intended move allows a check, capture, or tactical resource. Strategic plans work only when they survive concrete calculation.
