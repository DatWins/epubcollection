Introduction

A practical handbook for positional thinking, strategic planning, and converting advantages.

This book consolidates the chess concepts explored in our earlier lessons into one reference. It focuses on practical decision-making rather than memorizing isolated rules.

The central idea is simple: first make sure nothing urgent is being missed; then understand the position, identify a plan, improve your pieces, and calculate when the moment calls for concrete action.

How to use this book

  • Read the thought-process chapter first and practice its questions in your games.
  • Use the positional rules as reminders, not absolute laws.
  • Return to the temporary-versus-permanent chapter when deciding whether to act now or improve patiently.
  • Keep the final checklists nearby during review sessions.
Important: Tactics always take priority over a general strategic plan. A positional rule is useful only when it fits the actual position.
