12-Temporary Advantage - Bad Piece Position

Steinitz's Advantages: 12-Temporary Advantage - Bad Piece Position · 24 January 2019

Looking at Black's pieces, his a7 Rook, c8 Bishop, d6 Bishop, and f7 Knight are in bad positions. White has to play dynamically because this is a temporary advantage and Black will be looking at Bc5 to trade his bad bishop for White's good one, e6 to bring his c8 Bishop into the game; he will also go Kh8 to liberate himself from the pin
