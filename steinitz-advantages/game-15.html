15-Temporary Advantage - Concentration of Pieces in the Center (Centralization)

Steinitz's Advantages: 15-Temporary Advantage - Concentration of Pieces in the Center (Centralization) · 24 January 2019

White's centralization of his pieces is obvious. He has an advantage in the form of centralization. White's pieces have the advantage of being able to move from one flank to the other due to the nature of the centralization advantage. He must act quickly, though, because this is a temporary advantage and Black's plan will be to expel the pieces and trade off to get rid of his cramped position.
