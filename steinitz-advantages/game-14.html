14-Temporary Advantage - Advantage in Development

Steinitz's Advantages: 14-Temporary Advantage - Advantage in Development · 24 January 2019

White has given up three pawns in this position, but he has great compensation in the form of development. Again, in this type of position, White must play dynamically in order to convert his advantage into an attack or permanent advantage.
