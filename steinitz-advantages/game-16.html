16-Temporary Advantage - Space Advantage

Steinitz's Advantages: 16-Temporary Advantage - Space Advantage · 24 January 2019

White has an obvious space advantage. This affords him the luxury of maneuvering his pieces more easily, whereas Black's position is cramped. Again, this is a temporary advantage so White needs to play dynamically. He will be looking to bring his piece to their optimal squares to attack, using forcing moves.
